# Post-Merger Team Integration — Alignment Session
Participants: Helen (Integration Lead), David (Legacy Co. HR), Sophie (Acquired Co. HR), Mark (Legacy Co. Engineering Manager), Priya (Acquired Co. Engineering Manager)

Helen: Thanks everyone. Today we need to agree on how we integrate the two engineering teams. We've got 40 people from Legacy Co and 18 from the acquired team. The plan is to fold the acquired team into our existing structure — same processes, same tools, same reporting lines.

David: That makes sense. We have mature processes here. It's easier if everyone works the same way.

Mark: Agreed. Our CI/CD pipeline and sprint cadence are well established. Onboarding the new team onto our systems is the logical move.

Helen: Good. So we standardise on our stack, our tools, our ways of working. Sophie, Priya — you'll need to start the transition plans for your teams.

Sophie: I want to raise something before we finalise that. The acquired team has some pretty different ways of working that have produced strong results. Their deployment frequency is three times ours. Their incident rate is half.

Helen: That's a different context though. Smaller team, different product.

Mark: Our processes are designed for scale. What works for 18 people doesn't necessarily work for 40.

Priya: Some of our engineers are already nervous about this integration. A few of them joined specifically because of our engineering culture. If we ask them to abandon everything overnight—

Helen: Change is always uncomfortable. That's normal in a merger.

David: The sooner we standardise, the sooner we get to one culture. Dragging it out just prolongs the uncertainty.

Sophie: I'm not suggesting we drag it out. I'm suggesting we look at what's actually working before we decide what to standardise on.

Helen: We don't have time for a full audit. The board wants integration milestones by end of Q2.

Mark: We could lose months trying to evaluate two sets of processes. Let's just pick one and move forward.

Priya: I'm worried we're going to lose some of our best engineers over this.

Helen: Retention is always a risk in mergers. HR will handle that.

David: We have a standard retention package ready.

Sophie: A retention package doesn't fix a cultural fit problem.

Helen: Look, we need to make a decision today. Are we aligned on standardising to our existing processes?

Mark: Yes.

David: Yes.

Priya: I... yes. But I want it noted that I have concerns.

Sophie: Same. Noted concerns.

Helen: Noted. Let's move to the migration timeline.
