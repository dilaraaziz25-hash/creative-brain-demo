# Anchor Your Ambitions

This folder contains visual stimulus cards for the **Anchor** persona.

The Anchor pushes the team toward ambitious business outcomes, refusing to let creative ambition or commercial reality be diluted.

## What to Add

Drop one or more images (.jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp) into this folder. When the Anchor fires an intervention, the first image will be displayed as a visual reference.

Suggested imagery:
- Boardroom or strategic planning scenes
- North Star or ambitious goal imagery
- Mountain peaks or high-reaching visuals
- Leadership or vision-setting moments
- Scale or ambition metaphors

Maximum display height: 250px (auto-scaled to fit card width).
