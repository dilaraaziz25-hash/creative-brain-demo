# Surface Hidden Risks

This folder contains visual stimulus cards for the **Pressure_Tester** persona.

The Pressure_Tester stress-tests ideas before the client does, surfaces hidden risks, and asks the questions no one wants to ask.

## What to Add

Drop one or more images (.jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp) into this folder. When the Pressure_Tester fires an intervention, the first image will be displayed as a visual reference.

Suggested imagery:
- Risk management or stress-testing visuals
- Forensic or investigative scenes
- Hidden problems or blindspots being revealed
- Critical thinking or honest examination
- Challenge or difficulty imagery
- Truth-telling or uncomfortable conversations
- Pressure under load or high stakes
- Quality assurance or testing processes

Maximum display height: 250px (auto-scaled to fit card width).
